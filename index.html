Commands

Type / in any channel the bot can see and Discord lists these for you.

/stats

Look up a player's Donut SMP stats by username.

/worth

See what an item is worth on the auction house.

/ah

Browse the listings for an item on the auction house.

/price

The current lowest auction house price for an item.

/method

Estimate hourly, 12 hour and daily earnings for a farm or spawner.

/profit

Work out profit, margin and hourly rate for a flip or a farm.

/calculate

A calculator that understands k, m, b and t, so 1.5k and 2m just work.

/schematic render

Turn a .schem, .litematic or .nbt file into a 3D render with a material list and cost.

/vouch

Vouch for a trader, or report a scammer for staff to review.

/help

Every command and what it does.

Tickets

Set up a ticket panel from the /ticket dashboard, with transcripts saved to a channel you pick.

Welcome messages

Greet new members with your own title, colour and image from the /welcome dashboard.

Privacy and terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the Donut Utils Discord bot ("the bot") and this website. We keep it to what we need to make them work.

What we collect

  • Discord IDs: user IDs, server IDs and channel IDs, used to run commands and apply settings.
  • Command input: what you type into a command, such as a Minecraft username or item name, used to fetch your result.
  • Technical logs: timestamps, command names and error messages, used to fix bugs and keep the bot running.
  • Saved settings: anything a server or user chooses to save with the bot.

When you log in with Discord

The website asks Discord for your public profile only (the "identify" permission). We never see your email, your server list or your messages. When you sign in we save your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar, the date your account was created, whether and when you joined our support server, when you first signed in, and how many times you have signed in. Each sign-in is also posted to a private staff channel in our Discord server.

We set one cookie to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. It is removed when you log out.

Staff applications

If you apply for staff, your answers are saved and posted to a private staff channel with your Discord profile details so reviewers can read them. The decision and the reviewer's note are saved with the application, shown to you on the website, and sent to you in a Discord DM.

Schematic renderer

Files you upload to the renderer are processed in memory to make the images and material list. They are not written to disk or kept after the result is sent back.

What we don't collect

We don't collect passwords, payment details or private messages. The bot only reads messages that are commands for it.

How we use it

Data is used only to run the bot and website, review staff applications, fix problems and prevent abuse. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Third parties

The bot and the login run on Discord, and some lookups use outside data sources, so the text you submit with a command may be sent to them to get a result. Their own privacy policies apply, including Discord's.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server and kept only as long as needed. Logs are cleared regularly. If the bot is removed from a server, that server's saved data may be deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete the data tied to your Discord ID or your server, including your website sign-in record and any applications, by contacting us in the support server. You can also remove the bot from your server at any time, and you can log out of the website whenever you like.

Children

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement to use the bot or website. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions? Ask in the support server.
