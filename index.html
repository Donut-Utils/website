Commands

Type / in any channel the bot can see and Discord lists these for you.

/stats

Look up a player's Donut SMP stats by username.

/worth

See what an item is worth on the auction house.

/ah

Browse the listings for an item on the auction house.

/price

The current lowest auction house price for an item.

/calc farms

Pick a spawner or farm and get hourly, 12 hour and daily earnings as an image. Set your own price with the Change price button.

/profit

Work out profit, margin and hourly rate for a flip or a farm.

/calc expression

A calculator that understands k, m, b and t, so 1.5k and 2m just work.

/schematic render

Turn a .schem, .litematic or .nbt file into a 3D render with a material list and cost.

/vouch add

Vouch for a trader you have dealt with.

/vouch scam

Report a scammer. The bot DMs you for proof and staff review it.

/vouch leaderboard

See your vouches, or another user's, and the top vouched.

/bjgamble

Play a game of blackjack.

/pgamble

Play a game of paper.

/agamble

Play the arrow game. Land on a skull and you lose.

/pingprot

Give a user ping protection. Run it again to remove it.

/servers

See every server the bot is in, biggest first.

/help

Every command and what it does.
